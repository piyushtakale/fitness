# 📚 SDE2/SDE3 16-Week Elite Preparation Plan - Complete Index

## 🎯 Overview

This is your **complete 16-week preparation plan** for SDE2 and SDE3 roles at top tech companies. The plan is designed for someone with ~5 years of experience who wants to:

- Master DSA patterns (300+ problems)
- Become proficient in System Design (HLD + LLD + Machine Coding)
- Build a production-grade microservices project (Swiggy/Zepto clone)
- Leverage AI tools (Cursor, Copilot, RAG, LangChain) for elite development
- Actively apply for jobs and build a strong professional profile

## 📊 Track Summaries

### DSA Track (02_DSA_Track.md)

- **300+ problems** organized by pattern
- **16 patterns**: Arrays, Strings, Hashing, Two Pointers, Linked List, Trees, Binary Search, Backtracking, Heap, Intervals, Graph, DP, Greedy, etc.
- **Weekly target**: 25-30 problems (Weeks 1-8), 20-25 problems (Weeks 9-16)
- **Success metric**: 300+ problems by Week 16, confident with medium problems in <30 min

### HLD Track (03_HLD_Track.md)

- **20 problems** (10 frequent + 10 unique)

- **Unique**: Code Compiler, Stock Trading, Job Scheduler, Live Streaming, CDN, Hotel Aggregator, Gaming Backend, Ad Aggregator, Parking IoT, Collaborative Editor
- **Success metric**: Can design all 20 systems from scratch and defend trade-offs

### LLD Track (04_LLD_Track.md)

3. **AI-first development**: Use Cursor, Copilot, and AI agents to accelerate learning

5. **Apply early**: Start applying after Week 4, even if you don't feel "ready"
