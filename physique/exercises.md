Below is the complete copy-ready **six-day gym plan**: five lifting days, one cardio-and-abs day, plus your optional daily extra routine. Each lifting session is designed to stay within **45–60 minutes**.

## Weekly schedule

| Day | Session | Main focus |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Monday | Push | Chest, shoulders, triceps |
| Tuesday | Pull | Lats, upper back, rear delts, biceps |
| Wednesday | Legs A | Quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves |
| Thursday | Cardio + abs | Cardio fitness and direct core work |
| Friday | Upper V-taper | Lats, shoulders, upper chest, arms |
| Saturday | Legs B | Glutes, hamstrings, quads, calves |
| Sunday | Rest | Easy walking or mobility if desired |

Badminton is separate and optional. It does not replace or alter any planned gym workout.

## Monday: Push

| Order | Exercise | Equipment | Main muscles | Sets × reps |
| --: | :-- | :-- | :-- | --: |
| 1 | Dumbbell bench press | Flat bench + dumbbells | Chest, front delts, triceps | 2 × 8–12 |
| 2 | Incline dumbbell press | Adjustable bench + dumbbells | Upper chest, front delts, triceps | 2 × 8–12 |
| 3 | Seated dumbbell shoulder press | Bench + dumbbells | Front/side delts, triceps | 2 × 8–12 |
| 4 | Dumbbell lateral raise | Dumbbells | Side delts | 2 × 12–20 |
| 5 | Cable triceps pressdown | Cable machine + rope/bar | Triceps | 2 × 10–15 |
| 6 | Overhead cable triceps extension | Cable machine + rope | Triceps long head | 2 × 10–15 |

**Notes:** Keep your shoulder blades gently back during pressing movements. Use light, strict repetitions for lateral raises—no body swing.

## Tuesday: Pull

| Order | Exercise | Equipment | Main muscles | Sets × reps |
| --: | :-- | :-- | :-- | --: |
| 1 | Neutral-grip lat pulldown | Lat-pulldown machine | Lats, biceps, upper back | 2 × 8–12 |
| 2 | Seated cable row | Cable-row machine | Mid-back, lats, rhomboids, biceps | 2 × 8–12 |
| 3 | One-arm dumbbell row | Bench + dumbbell | Lats, upper back, biceps | 2 × 8–12 each side |
| 4 | Face pull | Cable machine + rope | Rear delts, traps, shoulder stabilizers | 2 × 12–15 |
| 5 | Dumbbell curl | Dumbbells | Biceps | 2 × 10–15 |
| 6 | Hammer curl | Dumbbells | Biceps, brachialis, forearms | 2 × 10–15 |

**Notes:** For pulldowns, think “elbows down toward the sides.” For rows, avoid swinging your torso or shrugging excessively.

| 2 | Supported dumbbell split squat | Dumbbells + rack/bench for balance support | Quads, glutes, hamstrings, balance | 2 × 8–12 each leg |
| 2A | **Alternative to split squat: leg press** | Leg-press machine | Quads, glutes, hamstrings | 2 × 10–12 |

| 6 | Standing calf raise | Calf-raise machine or body weight | Calves | 2 × 12–20 |

**Important:** Choose **either** supported split squats **or** leg press in Slot 2. Do not do both in the same workout at the start.

**Notes:** Start split squats bodyweight-only if needed. For Romanian deadlifts, hinge at the hips and keep dumbbells close to your legs.

## Friday: Upper V-taper

| Order | Exercise | Equipment | Main muscles | Sets × reps |
| --: | :-- | :-- | :-- | --: |
| 1 | Assisted pull-up or neutral-grip lat pulldown | Assisted pull-up machine or pulldown machine | Lats, biceps, upper back | 2 × 8–12 |
| 2 | Incline dumbbell press | Adjustable bench + dumbbells | Upper chest, front delts, triceps | 2 × 8–12 |
| 3 | Chest-supported row | Row machine or incline bench + dumbbells | Lats, mid-back, rear delts | 2 × 8–12 |
| 4 | Cable lateral raise | Cable machine | Side delts | 2 × 12–20 |
| 5 | Reverse pec deck or rear-delt fly | Machine or dumbbells | Rear delts, upper back | 2 × 12–15 |
| 6A | Hammer curl | Dumbbells | Biceps, forearms | 2 × 10–15 |
| 6B | Cable triceps pressdown | Cable machine | Triceps | 2 × 10–15 |

Exercises **6A and 6B are a superset**:

1. Perform one set of hammer curls.
2. Immediately perform one set of cable triceps pressdowns.
3. Rest 60–90 seconds.
4. Repeat the full pair once more.

That means **two sets of hammer curls and two sets of triceps pressdowns each**.

## Optional daily extra routine

This is separate from your gym workout. Keep it light enough that it does not reduce gym performance.


| Activity | Amount | Rule |
| :-- | --: | :-- |
| Push-ups | 20–30 total reps | Do not go to failure; reduce or skip on Monday if chest/triceps are fatigued |
| Pull-up practice | 2–3 light sets, 3–5 days/week | Use dead hangs, scapular pull-ups, assisted pull-ups, or controlled negatives |
| Crunches | 1 minute | Keep it easy on lifting days |
| Sit-ups | 1 minute | Controlled repetitions; stop if lower back feels irritated |
| Russian twists | 1 minute | Move slowly; do both sides evenly |
| Plank | 30–60 seconds | Keep hips level and ribs down |

On **Thursday**, do the structured abs workout above and skip this additional abs circuit, or keep it to only one easy round. Doing hard core work twice on the same day is unnecessary.

## Session rules

| Item | Starting rule |
| :-- | :-- |
| Warm-up | 5–10 minutes of easy cardio plus a few light warm-up sets before the first main lift |
| Working sets | Begin with 2 sets per exercise |
| Main exercise reps | Usually 8–12 repetitions |
| Isolation exercise reps | Usually 10–20 repetitions |
| Effort | Finish most sets with 2–3 good reps still possible |
| Rest: compound exercises | 90–120 seconds |
| Rest: smaller exercises | 45–75 seconds |
| Workout duration | 45–60 minutes |
| Pain | Stop an exercise if it creates sharp or joint pain; normal muscular fatigue is different |

## Program evolution

Keep this exercise selection stable for about **8–12 weeks**. The goal is not to find a new routine every month; it is to improve technique, repetitions, and weight on a small number of reliable movements.

For Weeks 1–2, use two sets and learn each exercise. From Week 3 onward, add weight only after you reach the top of the listed rep range with clean form. For example, when you can perform 12 controlled reps on both sets of dumbbell bench press, increase to the next available dumbbell weight and build again from 8–10 reps.

After 8–12 weeks, we can review performance, recovery, enjoyment, body measurements, and any exercises that feel uncomfortable. Then we can replace selected movements—not the entire program—when there is a practical reason. Current evidence supports progressively training major muscle groups at least twice weekly; heavier loads, full range of motion, and 2–3 challenging sets can improve strength, while greater weekly volume can support hypertrophy as your training capacity grows.[^1][^2]
